What is
Bazi 八字?

Bazi, or the Four Pillars of Destiny, is a time-honored discipline that reveals the interaction of Yin and Yang and the Five Elements through your year, month, day, and time of birth.

It offers a deeper understanding of your natural strengths, personal tendencies, and life patterns — so you can make more aligned decisions in life and business.
